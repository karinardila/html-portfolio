Karin's favorite movies

My top 3 movies ever and why I loved them

The Piano

The gloomy ambience and oppressing atmosphere of the restricted social rules on 19th century New Zealand had me rooting for the mute heroine to break free and go for her happy end right from the start. Also, Ada is a wonderful piano player and the soundtrack is otherworldly.

Lord of the Rings

I am a huge fan of Tolkien's writing and the movies did the books justice in my opinion. As a teenager I went to see each movie to the cinema three or four times and had quite a crush on Vigo Mortensen.

Pocahontas

Loved it as a kid and have a lot of nostalgia about it. As a girl, I identified with the adventurous indian princess who is in her element in the nature, makes friends with raccoons and such and jumps down the waterfalls whenever she feels like it. Still listen to the music occassionally.
