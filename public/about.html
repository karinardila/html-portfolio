About Me


I'm a happy web creator and a storyteller, a mom of two Pippi Langstrumpf sort of girls, a former hitchhiker & long-term slow traveler, a lover of mountains and hiking, a passionate reader, a wannabe polyglot (fluent in English, Spanish, French, Slovak and sarcasm).

I love obsessively taking photos (which I never look at again) and am allergic to cats. I tell stories in front of an audience for fun, sing very badly and want to learn to play ukulele.

I have a terrible memory of people's names and faces - if I don't seem to remember you, it's not personal! I'm also a very bad driver but not giving up.
